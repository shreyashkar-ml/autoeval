Two workflows

Experiment. Or optimize.

Both preserve the complete trail from source change to decision.

Standard experimentation compare variants

Compare focused variants, inspect sealed evidence, and turn the full run history into a recommendation.

Autoresearch optimize a metric

Freeze the evaluator, let the agent iterate, and keep or revert every scored attempt automatically.

How it works

Your agent proposes. Autoexp proves.

01

Define the boundary

Choose the objective, editable source, inputs, command, and evaluator.

02

Run and seal

Each proposal runs reproducibly with source, logs, metrics, and artifacts preserved.

03

Decide from evidence

A metric or your browser review keeps, reverts, or redirects the next attempt.
