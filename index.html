Autoresearch

Turn a metric into
a research loop.

Give the agent an objective and a fixed evaluator. It proposes one hypothesis at a time, runs it, reads the score, and keeps only real improvements—while you retain the full attempt ledger.

  • One explicit hypothesis per attempt
  • Metric history against baseline and best result
  • Improvements kept; misses automatically reverted
  • Source changes and decisions visible together
